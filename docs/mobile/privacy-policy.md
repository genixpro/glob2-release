# Globulation 2 Android privacy policy

This policy applies to the Android version of Globulation 2 (`org.globulation2.glob2`) distributed by the Globulation 2 project. For privacy questions, contact [bradley.allen.arsenault@gmail.com](mailto:bradley.allen.arsenault@gmail.com).

## Data on your device

The game saves your settings, maps, and saved games on your device so that you can continue playing. The app does not include advertising or analytics services and does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone. Android backup is disabled for the app. You can remove locally stored game data through Android's app storage controls or by uninstalling the app.

## Local network multiplayer

If you choose to host or join a game on a local network, the game exchanges your chosen player name, chat messages, and game information, including maps, with the host and other players. A host can see the network addresses of connecting players. LAN traffic is not encrypted, so use this feature only on a network and with players you trust. Other players or network operators may retain information they receive. The Globulation 2 project does not operate a server for LAN sessions and does not receive or retain this information from your LAN game.

## Online multiplayer

The YOG online sign-in option is unavailable in the current Android test build while the Globulation 2 server is offline. This build does not create or use an online account. We will update this policy and the Google Play data declarations before enabling online multiplayer in an Android release.

## Other network access

Opening this policy from the game launches your device's browser. That browser connects to the policy host under its own privacy terms. The game does not otherwise send gameplay, account, or diagnostic data to the Globulation 2 project during single-player play.

## Retention, deletion, and changes

We do not retain data from this Android build on a project-operated server. Local game data remains until you delete it from your device. Information you send to other LAN participants is controlled by those participants; ask them directly to delete any copies they retained. For privacy questions or requests, email the contact above. We will revise this policy when Android data practices change.
